# Licence of the files in this folder

These files are aggregate statistics computed from the CGMacros dataset (Das et al., Scientific Data, 2025; https://physionet.org/content/cgmacros/1.0.0/, DOI 10.13026/3z8q-x658). They contain no raw glucose series, photos or other participant-level records beyond per-participant summary sums.

Because they are derived from CGMacros, they are shared under the same licence as the dataset: [Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/). Please attribute CGMacros (Das et al.) and this repository, use them non-commercially, and share any adaptation under the same terms.
